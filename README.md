# SEO Audit Checklist (45 checks)

A practical, free SEO audit checklist for any website: crawling, on-page, technical, structured data, content, local and AI search. Tick the boxes as you go.

Full version with free tools and a printable PDF: **https://seovoro.com/seo-audit-checklist.php**

## Crawling and indexing

- [ ] robots.txt exists, does not block important pages and lists your sitemap (free tool: [Robots.txt Tester](https://seovoro.com/tools/robots-txt-tester.php))
- [ ] XML sitemap is valid and lists only canonical, indexable pages that return 200 (free tool: [Sitemap Validator](https://seovoro.com/tools/sitemap-validator.php))
- [ ] Sitemap is submitted in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools
- [ ] No important page is set to noindex by mistake
- [ ] Each page has one canonical URL, and duplicates point to it
- [ ] Status codes are right: 200 for live pages, 301 for moved pages, 404 or 410 for removed pages
- [ ] Important pages are reachable within about three clicks from the home page

## On-page basics

- [ ] Every page has a unique title tag of about 30 to 60 characters (free tool: [Title Tag Checker](https://seovoro.com/tools/title-tag-checker.php))
- [ ] Every page has a useful meta description of about 70 to 160 characters (free tool: [SERP Snippet Preview](https://seovoro.com/tools/serp-preview.php))
- [ ] Each page has one H1 that matches its topic
- [ ] Headings follow a logical order (H2, then H3)
- [ ] Images have descriptive alt text and sensible file names
- [ ] Internal links use clear anchor text and point to your important pages
- [ ] Content answers the search intent and is not copied or thin

## Technical health

- [ ] The site uses HTTPS, http redirects to https and only one www or non-www version is live
- [ ] No broken internal links (free tool: [Broken Link Checker](https://seovoro.com/tools/broken-link-checker.php))
- [ ] Redirect chains are removed so links go straight to the final page
- [ ] Pages are easy to read and use on a phone
- [ ] Largest Contentful Paint is 2.5 seconds or less on key pages (free tool: [Site Speed Tester](https://seovoro.com/tools/site-speed-tester.php))
- [ ] The layout does not jump while loading (CLS of 0.1 or less)
- [ ] Images are compressed and third-party scripts are kept to a minimum

## Structured data

- [ ] Organization schema on the home page with name, logo and profile links
- [ ] Breadcrumb schema on inner pages
- [ ] Article schema on articles, with author and dates
- [ ] LocalBusiness schema with accurate details, if you serve a local area
- [ ] FAQ or Product schema only where the page content really matches
- [ ] Test with the Rich Results Test and fix errors

## Content and trust

- [ ] Articles show author names and short bios; About and Contact pages are clear
- [ ] Claims are backed by sources, and the sources are linked
- [ ] Key pages show dates and are kept up to date
- [ ] Reviews and testimonials are genuine and used with permission
- [ ] Privacy policy, terms, refund and contact information are easy to find
- [ ] Pages include real examples, screenshots or data where possible

## Local SEO (if you serve a local area)

- [ ] Google Business Profile is claimed, verified and complete
- [ ] Name, address and phone are identical on your site and profiles
- [ ] Primary category and services are accurate
- [ ] There is a page for each main service and each area you genuinely serve
- [ ] Reviews are collected in a rule-following way and answered politely
- [ ] Opening hours, including holidays, are correct

## AI search and tracking

- [ ] Key pages open with a clear, direct answer, then explain
- [ ] Question-style headings and FAQ sections are used where helpful
- [ ] AI crawlers you want, such as GPTBot, are not blocked by mistake in robots.txt
- [ ] Brand details are consistent so systems can recognize who you are
- [ ] Search Console and Analytics are installed, and key actions such as calls and forms are tracked
- [ ] Search Console is reviewed monthly: clicks, impressions and indexing issues

## Notes

- This is general best practice. Your market and goals decide which checks matter most, and no checklist can promise a ranking.
